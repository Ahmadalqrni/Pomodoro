⏲️What is the Pomodoro Technique? ⏲️

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method where you focus for 25 minutes, then take a short break. After 4 sessions, you take a longer break.

☕Why we built this☕

We built Study With Me to help students stay focused and productive in a cozy, distraction-free environment.
